Since 1992, McBride's Steakhouse has welcomed Wichita Falls families, friends and steak lovers to the table. Our signature approach is simple: cut steaks in-house and cook them over 100% mesquite coals.

It's a tradition built around honest food, warm company and the pleasure of a great steak.
